Privacy Policy

EduPulse by Monotheist MW (`com.monotheistmw.edupulse`) • Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

EduPulse by Monotheist MW is a multi-school K-12 management platform operated on behalf of participating educational institutions. Each school acts as the Data Fiduciary for its own students, parents, and staff, and EduPulse processes school records solely to deliver attendance, daily homework diaries, examination marks, fee receipts, and school notices.

2. Information Collected

  • Student & Guardian Records: Admission number, student name, class, guardian name, guardian contact number, attendance records, exam marks, homework completion status, and fee payment/receipt history entered by authorized school staff.
  • Staff Records: Teacher name, timetable assignments, and optional GPS coordinates captured strictly when a teacher taps Punch In with Live GPS for staff attendance verification.
  • Authentication Data: School-issued login credentials verified through Google Firebase Authentication. No advertising identifiers, behavioral tracking profiles, or third-party ad SDKs are used.

3. Children's Privacy & Parental Consent (DPDP Act, 2023)

EduPulse is designed for K-12 schools in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Student records are created and managed only by authorized school administrators with guardian consent obtained during school admission. EduPulse does not display advertisements, track minors across apps or websites, or sell or share student information with third-party marketers.

4. Data Security & Isolation

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (Singapore region, asia-southeast1).
  • Server-enforced security rules deny all unauthenticated access, isolate every school into its own private workspace, and restrict parent accounts strictly to their own child's records.
  • Android device backup extraction (allowBackup) and cleartext HTTP traffic are disabled in the app manifest.

5. Data Retention, Correction & Deletion

Academic and fee records are retained for the period required by the school and applicable education-board regulations. Parents or guardians may request correction, export, or erasure of their child's personal data at any time through their school administration office or by contacting the platform administrator below.
